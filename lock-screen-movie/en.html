Your favorite video, on your lock screen.

Every time you pick up your phone, a treasured memory or a favorite view comes to life. Give your lock screen a video while leaving your home screen exactly as it is.

What it does

Actual screens from the app

Video on your lock screen

The video you choose plays as your lock screen background.

Apply changes instantly

On supported Android 14+ devices, switch videos without reopening Android settings.

Random playback

Each time the lock screen appears, one of your chosen videos plays at random (Android 14+).

Reuse earlier videos

Bring back a video you used before, with its settings intact.

7-day trial

Check every feature on your own device before buying.

Pay once, use it forever

No subscription, no auto-renewal. When the trial ends, the wallpaper you set keeps playing.

Details

  • Works on Android 8.0 or later. Setting a wallpaper for the lock screen alone, random playback, and applying without Android settings require Android 14 or later.
  • No permissions requested. No camera, location, or storage. It reads only the video you pick in the file picker.
  • Your videos never leave the device. The app connects only to complete a purchase; videos and settings are never transmitted.
  • Easy on the battery. Playback stops whenever the lock screen is not showing.
  • Your original file is untouched. Only up to 30 seconds from your start point is copied.
  • Right after a restart, a normal wallpaper is shown until you unlock the device once. This is how Android works.
